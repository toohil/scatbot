“Exploring Psychobiological Research Onboarding Conditions”


Thank you for taking part in this research project. 


1.What was the purpose of this study?
The study aimed to investigate how effective chatbots and human researchers are at helping participants understand research procedures.


2. What happens now that I have completed this study?
Your responses and survey answers will be anonymised 9 days after follow up to compare participants' understanding, comfort and experience and confidence after onboarding through the chatbot or human researcher. This data will then be used for Andrea Marshall, Lily McDonell, Saoirse O’Donovan and Dylan O’Toole’s dissertations.


3.What if I want to withdraw?
If you subsequently wish to withdraw from this research, you can contact a member of the research team within 9 days by email and provide their ID. After this time your data will be fully anonymised for analysis so it will not be possible to identify for  withdrawal.


4. How can I find out more about the study results?
Participants can hear the results of this research at the research day presentations at the School of Computer Science Open Day on Wednesday April 1st . This will take place at the Western Gateway Building, University College Cork, T12 XF62. 


Any future public outputs from the research, such as conference presentations and journal papers will be made available via the University College Cork’s institutional repository, CORA, or in summary on the website beats-lab.com.


Thank you again for your time.
